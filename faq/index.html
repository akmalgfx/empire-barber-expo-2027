Expo FAQ. Dates, tickets, booths and the venue.

Your questions

What is the Empire Barber Expo?
A barber and hair expo in Calgary, Alberta, Canada, run by Empire Barber Supply. It brings stage education, a trade show floor, competitions and awards into one day. 2027 is Part 3.
When and where is the 2027 expo?
Sunday, May 2, 2027 at the Big Four Roadhouse, Stampede Park, 1801 Big Four Trail SE, Calgary, Alberta. See the venue page.
Who is the expo for?
Barbers, hairstylists and the brands that supply them.
When do tickets go on sale?
November 9, 2026. Join the waitlist and we'll email you the ticket link.
Is there a free pass?
Yes. The first 10 people to click the link in the ticket-drop email get a free pass.
Can I book a vendor booth?
Yes. Every 10 × 10 booth comes with drape, a skirted table, two chairs and three expo passes. See the floor plan and request a booth.
What is included in a vendor booth?
An 8 ft black backwall drape, 3 ft black sidewall drape, a 6 ft black skirted table, 2 folding chairs, a wastebasket and 3 expo access passes.
How do vendors get electrical?
Electrical is sold separately through the Global Convention Services vendor portal.
Can I ship merchandise to the expo?
Yes. Send your merchandise to the organizers and it is delivered to the venue free of charge.
When is vendor set-up?
Saturday, May 1, 2027, the day before the expo.
How big was the 2026 expo?
Part 2 was held on Sunday, April 12, 2026 at the Big Four Roadhouse with 45 vendors on the floor. See the past expos.
How do I contact the team?
Email info@empirebarber.ca or call 587-889-2551.
