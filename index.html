About the expo

The details

What is the Empire Barber Expo?
An expo for barbers and hairstylists in Calgary, run by Empire Barber Supply. It brings stage education, a trade show floor, competitions and awards into one day. 2027 is Part 3.
When and where is the 2027 expo?
Sunday, May 2, 2027 at the Big Four Roadhouse, Stampede Park, 1801 Big Four Trail SE, Calgary.
When do tickets go on sale?
November 9, 2026. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you the ticket link. The first 10 people to click it get a free pass.
Can I book a vendor booth?
Yes. Every 10 × 10 booth comes with drape, a skirted table, two chairs and three expo passes. See the floor plan and request a booth.
How do I contact the team?
Email info@empirebarber.ca or call 587-889-2551.
